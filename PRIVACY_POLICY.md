# Touch Grass Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

Touch Grass is a personal digital wellness app designed to help users become more mindful of distracting app usage. This Privacy Policy explains how Touch Grass handles information.

## Information We Collect

Touch Grass does **not** require an account and does **not** collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, browsing history, or messages.

## Screen Time and App Selection Data

Touch Grass uses Apple’s Screen Time APIs, including Family Controls and Device Activity, so users can select apps they personally want to monitor.

When you select apps to monitor:

- App selections are handled through Apple’s system app picker.
- Touch Grass receives Apple-provided app/category tokens, not readable lists of your private app activity.
- Your selected app tokens and monitoring preferences are stored locally on your device and shared only with the app’s local monitor extension through an Apple App Group.
- Touch Grass does not upload your selected apps, usage activity, or Screen Time data to external servers.

## Notifications

Touch Grass may request permission to send notifications. Notifications are used only to deliver gentle nudges when selected usage thresholds are reached.

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

## Data Storage

Touch Grass stores app preferences locally on your device, such as:

- Whether onboarding has been completed
- Selected monitoring interval preferences
- Whether monitoring is enabled
- Apple Screen Time app/category selection tokens

This information is stored locally using Apple-provided storage mechanisms. Touch Grass does not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties.

## Analytics and Tracking

Touch Grass does **not** use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Touch Grass does **not** track you across apps or websites.

## Data Sharing

Touch Grass does not sell your data and does not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Data may be processed locally by Apple’s iOS frameworks to provide Screen Time and notification features.

## Children’s Privacy

Touch Grass is intended for personal digital wellness use. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can control Touch Grass permissions through iOS Settings:

- Disable notifications
- Revoke Screen Time authorization
- Delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
